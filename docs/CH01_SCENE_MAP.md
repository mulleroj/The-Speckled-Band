## Seven existing evidence keys
- `ticket`: partial return train ticket.
- `mud`: fresh mud on left sleeve.
- `fear`: visitor trembling and saying she is afraid.
- `family`: Helen's home at Stoke Moran.
- `sister`: unexplained death of Julia two years before.
- `whistle`: low whistle reported before Julia's death.
- `lastwords`: Julia's phrase, meaning unknown.

## Source fidelity checkpoints
The original opening has Holmes waking Watson and Helen Stoner recounting her story. Sherlock notices a return ticket in her left glove and mud on her left sleeve. Julia heard a low whistle before she died. In an adapted game, keep the key observations and uncertainty; simplify English without changing cause/effect. Helen is not guilty because she is frightened. Details held for later should not reveal the canonical solution prematurely.
