## Approved visual direction

The series style is a **color Victorian book-illustration style**: restrained, muted and painterly, with cultivated greens, warm brown-greys, ivory and cream, restrained burgundy, charcoal/ink darks, and natural gentle skin tones. The period black-and-white illustration tradition informs line, gesture and composition, not absence of color. The style is neither monochrome nor sepia-only and must remain compatible with the dark-green interface.
