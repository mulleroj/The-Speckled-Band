## A. Purpose

Roylott Master v01 is the approved identity anchor for Dr. Grimesby Roylott in *The Speckled Band*. The exact repository PNG must be the primary image reference for every future Roylott variant and scene illustration. This approval does not approve those future images or runtime use.

## B. Character goal

Depict a physically imposing, volatile and dangerous human being without turning him into a comic-book villain, monster or supernatural figure. Keep the design grounded in the 1892 story and the approved portrait. Do not telegraph the case solution in character art.

## C. Separate source description from visual adaptation

**Facts in the 1892 story:** Roylott is described as huge and very tall, broad enough to fill a doorway, with a large face marked by wrinkles and sun, deep-set eyes and immense strength. Helen reports that his anger is uncontrolled and that he has behaved violently. These facts may guide story-supported scenes.

**Author-approved visual choices in Master v01:** an age impression around sixty; broad-shouldered, powerful proportions; the portrait's specific lined face and penetrating gaze; swept-back greying hair; a neat dark moustache with grey; dark Victorian coat; green-brown waistcoat; and the colored, muted, painterly rendering. The story does not specify these exact age, hair, moustache or waistcoat choices. Preserve them because the portrait is approved, not because they are claims about Doyle's text.

## D. Binding appearance from approved v01

- **Age and build:** around sixty in appearance; tall, broad-shouldered and powerfully built.
- **Face:** preserve the exact deeply lined, weathered approved likeness, deep-set eyes and penetrating gaze.
- **Hair and moustache:** swept-back greying hair and neat dark moustache with grey; preserve their approved shape.
- **Clothing:** dark Victorian coat and green-brown waistcoat as the baseline.
- **Character:** authoritative, dangerous and volatile, but human and non-supernatural.
- **Rendering:** colored, muted, painterly Victorian book illustration, consistent with the other approved M05.2 masters and M05.1.

## E. What may vary

Pose, head angle, expression within character, gesture, source-supported props, light and background may change for an approved shot. The steel poker is scene-specific, not a permanent prop. Threatening gestures belong only in the supported Baker Street confrontation. No image may add solution-revealing details, unsupported clues, supernatural features or ethnic caricature.

Every future prompt must use `assets/visual/masters/characters/roylott/char.case01.roylott.master__v01__approved.png` as its **primary image reference**. A secondary pose reference must not override his identity.
