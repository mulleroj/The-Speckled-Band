## Historical and safeguarding context

The original story uses a Victorian racist label for Roma people and treats their clothing and presence as a suspect theory. The learner adaptation deliberately omits this unreliable association. In a teacher-led history discussion, explain that the source reflects discriminatory attitudes of its period; distinguish quotation for critical analysis from endorsement. Do not ask Roma learners to represent or explain a community.

Discuss Helen's fear and wrist marks without graphic images, forced disclosure, victim-blaming, or assuming a learner has personal experience. Her testimony is her own; Watson notices, asks respectfully, and listens. Teacher Mode is not a safety service. If a learner raises a real safeguarding concern, use the school's established safeguarding process outside the game.
