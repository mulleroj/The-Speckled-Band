## Historical and safeguarding context

The original story uses a Victorian racist label for Roma people and speculates about their clothing and presence as part of the mystery. Chapter II learner copy deliberately omits that association: it is not reliable evidence and must not be repeated as a clue or culprit lead. In a teacher-led history discussion, explain that the source reflects discriminatory attitudes of its period; distinguish quoting a historical stereotype for critical analysis from endorsing it. Do not ask Roma learners to represent or explain a community.

Helen's fear and wrist marks should be discussed without sensational images, victim-blaming, forced disclosure, or assuming that a learner has personal experience. Her testimony is her own; Watson's role is to notice, ask respectfully, and listen. Teacher Mode is not a safety service. If a learner raises a real safeguarding concern, follow the school's established safeguarding process outside the game.

- These notes are keyed to scene/challenge IDs and are invisible in learner mode.
