# Chapter II — Evidence plan

Four proposed Chapter II registry records. These are content/state proposals only; do not add them to the runtime registry before M04 authorization. `observation`, `reported`, and `inference` are distinct: a clue record preserves its source; a learner's inference is an answer/reasoning event, not silently converted into an observed fact.

## Grant and source safeguards

- Required evidence is event-granted, idempotent, and independent of challenge correctness and Watson's optional response wording.
- Chapter I evidence remains owned by Ch. I. Optional recap can display validated historical records under their original provenance but cannot write them into Chapter II's found-evidence collection.
- Helen's explanation of the wrist marks is testimony in dialogue. The one requested wrist record remains an observation; the challenge labels the statement as Helen's report. Do not merge her explanation into a falsely direct observation.
- The total/allowance figures are uttered by Holmes after his research. The source of the information is “Holmes's report of a will calculation.” Do not imply Watson was at Doctors' Commons or inspected the document.
- Every record displays a source scene, source kind, and chapter. Do not use culprit-revealing filenames, alt text, hidden answer labels, or Teacher Mode data in learner-facing UI.
- The 1892 story's Roma-related statements are not new evidence for this chapter. Do not add a record about the community or offer an ethnic group as a culprit hypothesis.
