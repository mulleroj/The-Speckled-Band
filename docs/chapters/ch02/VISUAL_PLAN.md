## Art and accessibility rules

- Preserve one stable Baker Street layout and character appearance across S01–S05; update staging and props without implying different locations.
- Do not show a graphic injury, forced touch, restraint, or a frightened helpless Helen. Her agency, expression, and consent to show her wrist remain visible.
- Roylott's physical power is evidenced by the canonical poker action; avoid animalistic features, racial coding, or unrelated threatening props.
- Keep the poker, wrist, and notes visually descriptive, not answer-revealing. Do not use a snake/animal/room-vent image, solution-coding filename, alt text, tooltip, or hidden DOM label.
- Every illustration receives a concise factual alt text. Decorative atmosphere uses empty alt text. Offer the same story facts in text; no clue is available only through image recognition.
- Assets should be optimized, responsive, and non-blocking. No motion is necessary; honor reduced-motion settings if an approved later treatment adds motion.
- Do not infer a visual master or final path from these logical slot IDs. Update the media manifest only after asset approval.
