# M05.2 — Sherlock Holmes Master Portrait Brief

## A. Purpose

The first identity-defining master portrait of Sherlock Holmes for *The Baker Street Cases* has been approved as **Master v01**. Its author-provided image is the binding reference for later approved views and scene illustrations. It is not yet approved for runtime use.

## B. Character goal

Holmes should read as tall, slim, sharply featured, highly intelligent, reserved, analytical and elegantly dressed without vanity. He is a thoughtful literary detective, not a caricature, film action hero or theatrical costume shorthand. A deerstalker is not his default, especially indoors. A pipe is not required in every version and should appear only if separately approved for a specific view.

## C. Proposed visual interpretation

The first portrait has resolved these appearance choices. The 1892 story does not settle most of them; they are approved adaptation choices recorded in `M05_2_SHERLOCK_LOCK.md`.

- **Age impression:** adult in his mid-thirties, approximately 34–38; composed and experienced, not prematurely old or boyish.
- **Face:** long, narrow oval with a high forehead, defined cheekbones and lean cheeks; natural asymmetry and fine expression lines, not gauntness.
- **Nose and jaw:** long, straight-to-slightly aquiline nose; clean, narrow jaw with a firm but not exaggerated chin.
- **Brow:** dark, defined brows with a slight natural asymmetry; analytical focus comes from attention and gaze, not a permanently furrowed scowl.
- **Eyes:** muted brown-hazel as visible in Master v01; clear, observant, naturally proportioned, no glow or uncanny saturation.
- **Hair:** dark brown, neat and brushed back with a subtle natural wave; no dramatic curl, theatrical forelock, or modern sculpted styling.
- **Expression:** neutral, self-possessed and attentive; mouth relaxed, gaze thoughtful and slightly off-camera as if considering a detail. No smile is required, but do not make him hostile.
- **Posture:** upright, slender shoulders, slight forward inclination of the head; controlled and economical. Hands, if visible, rest naturally and do not point at an invented clue.
- **Interior clothing:** dark Victorian coat over a green-grey/charcoal waistcoat, high-collared ivory shirt and restrained muted burgundy neckwear, as shown in Master v01. Elegant tailoring, practical fit, no medals or decorative excess.
- **Texture and tone:** colored painterly book illustration; visible but restrained line and selective hatching, soft pigment transitions, natural skin texture. Use charcoal, green-grey, warm brown-black, ivory and a very small muted burgundy or antique-gold accent. Keep color muted, not monochrome or sepia-only.
- **Lighting/background:** soft plausible interior key light with gentle warm/cool balance; simple deep green-grey, warm neutral or softly suggested Baker Street background. Background remains subordinate and contains no readable text or clue props.

## D. Color direction

Use dark neutral clothing in charcoal, green-grey and brown-black; ivory collar and shirt; natural, rather pale but healthy skin; and only a restrained warm or muted burgundy accent. Balance cool ambient interior shade with a gentle warm reflected light. Avoid saturated blue, bright scarlet, pure black crush, orange sepia wash and pale skin rendered as white.

## E. Shot definition

**Approved v01 shot:** a seated, painterly portrait with a thoughtful hand near the chin, dark textured coat, green-grey waistcoat, ivory high collar and restrained burgundy tie, against a softly detailed Baker Street interior. This pose and background are specific to v01 and are not mandatory in later variants. See the attached reference identified in `M05_2_SHERLOCK_LOCK.md`.

The master establishes identity. A separate neutral front view may still be requested if needed to extend the reference set; do not treat one image as a complete turnaround.

## F. Non-negotiable rules

**Must remain stable after the master is approved:** apparent age, face proportions, nose and jaw, brow and eye placement, hairline and hairstyle, build/silhouette, base clothing palette and cut, and the restrained illustrated rendering style. Later view angles and scene lighting may not redesign these identity features.

**May vary by approved shot:** camera angle, body pose, gaze direction, light temperature/intensity, a small expression shift, coat state when supported by the scene, and whether hands or a story-required prop are visible. Hats and pipe are optional scene-specific choices, never defaults.

**Binding in approved v01:** facial likeness, age impression, hair, clothing baseline, palette, proportions and overall character, as listed in `M05_2_SHERLOCK_LOCK.md`. Revisions to a binding trait require creator approval of a new master version, not silent drift.
