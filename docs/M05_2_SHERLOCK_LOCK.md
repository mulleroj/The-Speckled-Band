## Approved likeness

The approved image depicts an adult man in his mid-to-late thirties, tall and slim, with a narrow, angular, sharply defined face; dark, brushed-back, softly waved hair; a calm, observant analytical gaze; and a cultivated, reserved expression. His clothing is a dark Victorian coat, green-grey/charcoal waistcoat, high ivory shirt and restrained muted burgundy neckwear. The color treatment is muted, elegant, painterly and literary, consistent with the locked M05.1 visual direction. The image's seated posture, hand-at-chin gesture, room background and exact light are features of this portrait, not required identity traits.

## Binding continuity traits

The following are now binding for future Sherlock images unless the creator explicitly approves a new master version:

- **Face:** the approved narrow angular likeness and its facial proportions/features.
- **Age impression:** adult, approximately 34–38.
- **Hair:** dark, brushed back, softly waved as in Master v01; preserve hairline and silhouette.
- **Base clothing:** dark Victorian coat, green-grey/charcoal waistcoat, high ivory shirt; keep tailoring, palette and period character coherent.
- **Color treatment:** restrained colored Victorian book illustration, muted and painterly; no monochrome, sepia-only, photorealistic or comic redesign.
- **Overall character:** intelligent, analytical, calm, cultivated and reserved; never a caricature or action hero.

## Elements allowed to vary by approved brief

- Pose and camera angle.
- Hands and gestures.
- Expression intensity within the approved calm, analytical range.
- Background and location context.
- Lighting direction, temperature and intensity, while keeping the face legible and colors naturally muted.
- Props only when the particular scene supports them; a pipe or deerstalker is never automatic.

## Continuity rule

All future Sherlock portraits, poses, expressions and scene illustrations must use the author-approved Master v01 image as the identity reference. Preserve the binding traits above; vary only the listed elements when the shot brief calls for them. A change to a binding trait requires creator approval of a new master version. Do not overwrite v01.
