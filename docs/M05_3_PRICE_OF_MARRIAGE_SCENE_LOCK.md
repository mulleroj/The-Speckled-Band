## Scene note

Holmes returns carrying a blue sheet with financial figures while Watson listens. The sheet represents Holmes's research notes; it is not the will itself and must not be presented as a readable legal document. The reported figures can support an inference about a possible financial motive, but motive is not proof of guilt or of what caused Julia's death. Preserve the unresolved mystery and introduce no premature spoilers or unsupported evidence.
