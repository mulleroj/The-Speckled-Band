## A. Shared identity and style block

Include this requirement in every future Watson image prompt:

> Use the repository image `assets/visual/masters/characters/watson/char.series.baker-street-cases.watson.master__v01__approved.png` as the **main image reference** for identity. Preserve that exact Watson's face and facial proportions, apparent age (approximately 35–40), naturally robust build, brown period-styled hair, neat brown moustache, brown Victorian coat and waistcoat, light high-collared shirt, dark blue necktie, dignified intelligent kind bearing, and warm natural coloring. Render in the approved restrained, muted, colored, painterly Victorian book-illustration style of M05.1. Pose references, if any, are secondary and may guide pose only; they must never replace or override the Watson Master v01 likeness. Do not redesign the character.

## B. Planned variant prompts

Each draft below requires the exact primary reference above. Prompts are plans, not authorization to generate.

### 1. Neutral portrait

Use the repository Watson Master v01 PNG named above as the **main image reference**. Create a calm neutral portrait of the same Watson, framed chest-up or mid-torso, with face clearly readable and a quiet, dignified, intelligent, kind expression. Preserve the approved likeness, hair, moustache, age, build, base clothing and palette exactly. Use a simple low-detail warm brown-grey or muted green-grey background and soft plausible interior light. Do not include the master portrait's notebook, pen or seated pose unless separately requested. No other person, readable text, clue, border, logo, photorealism or redesign.

### 2. Standing pose

Use the repository Watson Master v01 PNG named above as the **main image reference**. Show the same Watson standing naturally, framed from head to upper thigh, so his robust proportions and brown Victorian coat and waistcoat are clear. Preserve the exact approved face, age impression, brown period-styled hair, neat brown moustache, light high collar, dark blue necktie and painterly color treatment. Use a relaxed, balanced stance and restrained background. Do not carry over the seated writing pose unless the brief requires it. No dramatic gesture, unsupported prop, extra person or costume redesign.

### 3. Listening to Holmes

Use the repository Watson Master v01 PNG named above as the **main image reference**. Depict the same Watson listening attentively to Holmes, framed as a medium portrait or two-person scene only if Holmes is separately supplied as a scene reference. Watson's gaze and posture show attention and thought, not fear or comic confusion. Preserve Watson's exact approved likeness, clothing baseline, build and style. Do not change his identity to match Holmes; any Holmes reference is secondary and applies only to Holmes. Do not add dialogue text, a speech balloon, answer-bearing document or unsupported clue.

### 4. Writing / narrating

Use the repository Watson Master v01 PNG named above as the **main image reference**. Show the same Watson writing a private case note or narrating in a quiet, natural pose. The notebook and pen may appear only because this shot calls for writing; keep all pages blank or illegible. Preserve his face, hair, moustache, apparent age, build, clothing and warm muted painterly style. The seated study setup is optional and must not be copied unless requested. No readable writing, invented evidence, extra person or redesign.

### 5. Concerned expression

Use the repository Watson Master v01 PNG named above as the **main image reference**. Create a restrained concerned expression variant of the same Watson: a slight tension around the eyes or mouth, humane attention and composure. Preserve the approved facial structure, age, hair, moustache, build, base clothes and palette. Do not turn concern into panic, melodrama, menace or a new likeness. Keep face readable and background quiet; no unsupported clue or readable text.

### 6. Investigation scene

Use the repository Watson Master v01 PNG named above as the **main image reference** for Watson's identity. Place the same Watson in a text-supported investigation scene, observing or recording a specific clue only when that clue and action are supplied by an approved scene brief. Preserve the approved face, age, hair, moustache, build, clothing baseline and illustration style. Any location, prop, other character or clue must follow its own approved reference and the story source; do not invent evidence or signal an inference as an observed fact. Keep important action legible and the background subordinate. No readable text, horror treatment or character redesign.

## C. Shared exclusions

Exclude modern film or television styling; photorealism; glossy 3D; comic panels or speech balloons; anime; caricature; oversaturated color; monochrome or sepia-only treatment; an excessively young or elderly face; altered facial proportions; missing or redesigned moustache; modern hairstyle; slim Holmes-like body; changed clothing palette; military/action-hero posing; unsupported weapons, clues, writing, watermark, logo or frame; and any effect that makes the illustration uncanny or melodramatic.

## D. Continuity rule

The canonical Watson Master v01 PNG is the primary identity reference for every future image. Preserve its approved likeness and baseline features. A pose image can be secondary guidance only. Correct drift against the canonical master; never promote a drifted variant into the identity baseline or overwrite v01. Each future variant and scene-use image needs its own creator review.
