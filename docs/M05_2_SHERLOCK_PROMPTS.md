## A. Master prompt — neutral portrait

**Historical draft only:** this was prepared before the approved reference was supplied. Do not use it to replace or regenerate Master v01. Master v01 is the continuity anchor below.

Create an original, color Victorian literary book illustration of Sherlock Holmes for *The Baker Street Cases*, an 1880s-set Sherlock Holmes adaptation. This is the first identity-approval master, not a scene illustration. Depict one adult man in his mid-thirties, tall and slim, framed from mid-torso up in a calm half-length portrait. Turn his shoulders about 20–30 degrees from the viewer and keep his face clearly readable; eye-level camera, modest headroom, shoulders and hair fully inside frame.

Give him a long narrow oval face, high forehead, defined but natural cheekbones, lean cheeks, a long straight-to-slightly aquiline nose, a narrow firm jaw, dark defined brows with subtle asymmetry, and observant grey-hazel or muted grey-brown eyes. His expression is neutral, reserved and analytical: relaxed mouth, thoughtful gaze slightly off-camera, no scowl and no grin. His dark-brown hair is neatly brushed back with a subtle natural wave, period-plausible and understated.

Dress him in an elegant but practical dark Victorian indoor coat, charcoal or green-grey waistcoat, high-collared ivory shirt and restrained dark neckwear. Use charcoal, softened green-grey, warm brown-black, ivory and only a tiny muted burgundy or antique-gold accent. Keep skin naturally pale but healthy. Light him with soft plausible interior light, a restrained warm reflection balanced by cool green-grey ambient shade. Use a quiet deep green-grey or warm neutral background with only vague, low-detail room atmosphere.

Render as an original painterly colored book illustration: confident economical ink-like line, expressive but controlled gesture, selective fine hatching and subtle pigment texture. The historical black-and-white illustration tradition informs the line and composition, while the image remains visibly colored. Elegant, intelligent and quietly atmospheric. Prioritize consistent facial identity, silhouette, posture, collar and coat. No other people, no action, no readable writing, no clue props, no border, no caption, no logo. Do not make a final scene asset.
