## B. Character goal

Watson is a capable Victorian doctor and observer: dignified, intelligent, warm and humane. His naturally more robust build provides a clear contrast with Holmes while remaining realistic. He is neither a comic foil nor a military caricature. Preserve the approved likeness rather than inventing a new interpretation.

- **Face and expression:** retain the exact approved facial likeness; dignified, intelligent and kind in bearing.
